The named sigil, item, and trait lookup tables are reduced factual mappings derived from these projects:

- [Nenkai/GBFRDataTools](https://github.com/Nenkai/GBFRDataTools/tree/6783d753ef2beb8922e0419747bd972c68768ad8): Sigil and item names and ID hashes from `GBFRQuestClearChecker/csv_data/item_id.csv`. The MIT license is reproduced in [`licenses/GBFRDataTools-MIT.txt`](licenses/GBFRDataTools-MIT.txt).
- [X-Zero-L/relink-save-forge](https://github.com/X-Zero-L/relink-save-forge/tree/4f9e2ea0345bdde997c707a239932fc7e0387374): 2.0.2 stackable item hashes and `TXT_ITEM` keys from `catalogs/stackable-items-2.0.2.json`; used to fill 99 current item hash entries in `src/material-catalog.js`. The MIT license is reproduced in [`licenses/relink-save-forge-MIT.txt`](licenses/relink-save-forge-MIT.txt).
- [KrisCris/GBRelink-Sigil-Wiki](https://github.com/KrisCris/GBRelink-Sigil-Wiki/tree/93add042ed840d92bf2b0667355bc0b4773dfc17): Sigil-to-trait mappings, trait names, and the Japanese, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese Sigil/trait labels in `src/inventory-terms.js`, derived from `outputs/sigil-database.json`. The MIT license is reproduced in [`licenses/GBRelink-Sigil-Wiki-MIT.txt`](licenses/GBRelink-Sigil-Wiki-MIT.txt).
- The four Wrightstone type/hash and primary-trait mappings are based on [`BitterG/GBFR-PE-Patch-Tool`](https://github.com/BitterG/GBFR-PE-Patch-Tool/blob/b67bce7704719e331359c1a3393ec8f090bcc192/internal/backend/data/wrightstones.json) and its trait catalog. That repository had no `LICENSE` file at the inspected revision; no source code or legality rules from it are included here.

The Japanese labels in `src/material-terms.js` are a best-effort cross-reference to Game8's [Relink and Endless Ragnarok item list](https://game8.jp/gbf-relink/586845), Altema's [item list](https://altema.jp/grablurelink/itemlist), and the [Huiji item table](https://gbf.huijiwiki.com/wiki/Data%3A%E7%89%A9%E5%93%81%E5%88%97%E8%A1%A8.tabx), which has `name_en` and `name_jp` columns for shared Granblue items. These sites publish Japanese item names but do not provide a complete mapping to the Relink `TXT_ITEM` keys; some English-specific names therefore use readable Japanese translations. The English names and Japanese in-game item names remain associated with Cygames and are not relicensed by this project.
